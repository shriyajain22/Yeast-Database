How do I search for a protein?
Enter an ORF ID (e.g., YDL014W) or gene name (e.g., NOP1) in the search bar and click Search..
What data does this database contain?
Arginine methylation sites in S. cerevisiae proteins from three publications: Pang 2010, Low 2013, and Yagoub 2015.
How can I download data?
Go to the Search page, enter a term (or leave empty), and click Download CSV or TXT.
What do mono and di mean?
Mono = monomethylation (one methyl group), Di = dimethylation (two methyl groups).
What is Hmt1p?
The major arginine methyltransferase in yeast, responsible for methylating nucleolar and RNA-binding proteins.
Who do I contact for questions?
Dr. Michael Yu (mcyu@buffalo.edu) for data questions, or the BF768 course staff for technical issues.
